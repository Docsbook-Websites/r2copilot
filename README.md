# R2 Copilot: private AI for business

R2 Copilot is an AI assistant that works inside Microsoft Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote, in Chrome, in Telegram and in a browser chat. What makes it different from a public chatbot is where your text goes: content you type is encrypted on your device, travels through the patented SDNP network, and is discarded when you close the add-in. It is not retained to train a model.

These pages answer the four questions people ask before installing it: **what it costs**, **where my text goes**, **which apps it works in**, and **how to install it**.

## What R2 Copilot does differently

**Your content is encrypted before it leaves the device.** Text you compose and prompts you enter are encrypted on your device and immediately deleted when you close the add-in, so the content of your correspondence stays out of the public internet.

**No chat history is kept.** The assistant does not store chat history, and R2 does not store, collect or share user-generated content.

**The transport is the product.** Data packets are reordered, split, mixed and repeatedly encrypted as they pass through independent media nodes, under the patented Secure Dynamic Communication Network and Protocol. [Read how that works](./security/how-it-works.md).

**It runs where the work happens.** No separate window to paste into — the assistant is a panel inside the app you are already using.

# Supported apps

R2 Copilot is available today as Microsoft 365 add-ins for **Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote**, as a **Chrome extension**, as a **Telegram bot**, and as a **web chat**. Three marketplace listings cover the five Office apps — one for Outlook, one for Excel, and one shared by Word, PowerPoint and OneNote.

All Microsoft listings are published under **Stealth Mail**, the company behind R2 Copilot. If your procurement or security process asks who the publisher is, that is the name it will see — see [Compliance and certification](./security/compliance.md).

## One subscription, several apps

A subscription is tied to your account, not to a single app: the same plan covers the add-ins you have installed. Plan limits are shared — prompts and tokens come out of one pool no matter which app spends them. See [Plans and pricing](./pricing.md).

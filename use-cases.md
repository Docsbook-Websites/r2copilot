# Who uses R2 Copilot and for what

The common thread across these roles is the same sentence: *this text cannot go into a public assistant*. R2 Copilot exists for the work that is otherwise done by hand because the alternative is unacceptable.

## Legal

Keeping privilege intact while working with clients and other firms, and handling pleadings, judicial records and other documents that must remain undisclosed. Mostly in [Word](./apps/word.md) and [Outlook](./apps/outlook.md).

## Healthcare

Working with confidential patient information without it leaving the private space. See [Compliance and certification](./security/compliance.md) for what to request before a HIPAA review.

## Finance and banking

Protecting customer financial information, supporting regulatory compliance by reducing the risk of unauthorised access, and giving bank staff an assistant that handles customer data safely. Mostly in [Excel](./apps/excel.md).

## Insurance

Processing customer data and records securely while raising day-to-day throughput.

## Marketing

Developing concepts and campaigns without exposing product strategy — the case both published customer stories describe: [Donbas Energo](./cases/donbas-energo.md) and [Soleeva Energy](./cases/soleeva.md).

## Research and academia

Keeping unpublished scholarly work exclusive: drafts, data and argument stay out of anything that trains on them.

## Writers and screenwriters

Working on screenplays, books and stories that are not meant to become someone else's training material.

## Executives and entrepreneurs

Document flow, budget planning and secure exchange of data with an assistant — and the ability to give an entire staff a way to work with sensitive corporate information.

## Information security

An isolated communications environment for sensitive data, and a way to give employees an assistant instead of watching them use an unsanctioned one. Start with [What is stored and what is not](./security/data-handling.md).

## Analysts and data teams

Automating collection and interpretation, policy drafting and report preparation over data you would not paste elsewhere.

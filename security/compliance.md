# Compliance and certification

R2 Copilot's compliance position rests on two things: **it does not retain user content**, which removes the class of risk most privacy regulation is concerned with, and **it encrypts content in transit and at rest**, which is what the GDPR, HIPAA and CCPA claims are based on. Separately, the product holds **Microsoft Solutions Partner** status under the Industry AI on Azure program.

## Regulations named

R2 does not store, collect or share user-generated content, and uses data encryption to comply with a range of privacy regulations, including:

- **GDPR** — European Union general data protection regulation
- **HIPAA** — United States health information privacy
- **CCPA** — California consumer privacy

These are the regulations the vendor states the product is built to comply with. A formal attestation for your own audit file is requested through the [contact form](https://r2copilot.ai/contacts).

## Microsoft Solutions Partner

R2 Copilot for Marketing has attained **Microsoft Solutions Partner** status, certified under the **Industry AI on Azure** program. The certification covers a solution built to the Azure Well-Architected Framework, maintaining "Moderate" or higher scores across Reliability, Security and Operational Excellence, and running entirely within Azure.

The product pages also carry Microsoft Solution Partner designations for **Digital & App Innovation (Azure)**, **Data & AI (Azure)** and **Infrastructure (Azure)**.

## Who the publisher is

The Microsoft marketplace listings for the add-ins are published under **Stealth Mail**, and Microsoft's app certification page for R2 Copilot lists Stealth Mail as the partner company, with headquarters in Ukraine.

Stealth Mail and R2 Copilot are the same vendor: R2 Copilot is the product, and Stealth Mail is the company that publishes it in the Microsoft store. If your procurement checks match the publisher name against the contract counterparty, this is the page to point at.

## What is a certification and what is a design claim

Being explicit about this saves a round-trip during a review:

| Statement | Kind |
|---|---|
| Microsoft Solutions Partner, Industry AI on Azure | Certification held, verifiable with Microsoft |
| Azure Well-Architected scores of "Moderate" or higher | Assessment against a Microsoft framework |
| Runs in Azure with encryption at rest and RBAC | Architecture, described on [What is stored](./data-handling.md) |
| Complies with GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA | Vendor statement based on the above; ask for the attestation your auditor needs |
| SDNP and Last Mile Protection | Patented technology, see [How R2 keeps content private](./how-it-works.md) |

## For your vendor review

Request through the [contact form](https://r2copilot.ai/contacts): the data processing agreement, the processing region for your tenant, the sub-processor list, and written confirmation of deletion practice. Say which of your entities is contracting and which regulation your review is against — the answer differs for HIPAA and GDPR.

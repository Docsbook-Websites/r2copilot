# R2 Copilot pricing

R2 Copilot is sold as a monthly or annual subscription. Individual plans start at **$29 per month** for one user and about 250 prompts; enterprise plans start at **$79 per month** for three users and go up to **$2,559 per month** for 135 users. Every plan runs on a GPT language model, and the difference between them is how many prompts and tokens you get and how many people share them.

## Individual plans

For one user.

| Plan | Price | Included per month |
|---|---|---|
| Lite | $29 / month | ~250 prompts · 500,000 tokens |
| Standard | $39 / month | ~500 prompts · 1,000,000 tokens |
| Advanced | $49 / month | ~1000 prompts · 2,000,000 tokens |
| Ultimate | $99 / month | ~2500 prompts · 5,000,000 tokens |

An individual plan covers one person. If you run out of tokens, you either buy a new subscription or switch to a plan with more tokens and prompts. Unused tokens from the current subscription expire when it ends and do not transfer to a new one.

## Enterprise plans

For groups, with users included and add-ons priced per plan.

| Plan | Price | Included per month | Users | Extra user | Extra 200,000 tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Group | $79 / month | ~750 prompts · 1,500,000 tokens | 3 | $20 | $10 |
| Team | $249 / month | ~2500 prompts · 5,000,000 tokens | 10 | $16 | $9 |
| Business | $999 / month | ~12500 prompts · 25,000,000 tokens | 50 | $12 | $7 |
| Enterprise | $2,559 / month | ~33750 prompts · 67,500,000 tokens | 135 | $11 | $6 |

The larger the plan, the cheaper each additional user and token block: an extra seat costs $20 on Group and $11 on Enterprise.

Enterprise plans also include the ability to purchase additional prompts, and advanced protection and chat privacy.

## Monthly or annual

Both plan families have a monthly and an annual option; the annual option is the cheaper of the two per month. Switch between them with the **Monthly / Annual** toggle on the [pricing page](https://r2copilot.ai/pricing).

## How to read prompts and tokens

A **prompt** is one exchange with the assistant. A **token** is the unit the language model actually counts — roughly a word fragment, in both what you send and what comes back. The plan lists both because a long document summary consumes many more tokens than a one-line rewrite, so the prompt count is an estimate at typical length and the token number is the real ceiling.

Practically: pick a plan by tokens if you work with long documents, and by prompts if you mostly write short email replies.

## Which plan to start on

- **One person trying it out.** Lite, or the free trial first — see [Install and first run](./getting-started.md).
- **A team of three to ten.** Group or Team; the per-user price is lower than buying individual plans, and users come out of one pool.
- **A department with a security review.** Business or Enterprise, and read [What is stored and what is not](./security/data-handling.md) before the review meeting — it answers what the reviewer asks.

## Buying through the Microsoft marketplace

The add-in listings show a SaaS package at $29.00 per month with a site license also available. The full plan ladder is the one above. If your procurement runs through the Microsoft marketplace, the listing identifiers are in [Supported apps](./availability.md).

## What happens when you stop paying

The assistant stops working when the subscription ends. Nothing else has to be cleaned up: R2 does not store chat history or user-generated content, so there is no archive of conversations to delete afterwards. Data held about your account can be deleted at any time — see [What is stored and what is not](./security/data-handling.md).

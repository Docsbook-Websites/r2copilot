# Getting help

Questions go through the [contact form](https://r2copilot.ai/contacts). It asks for your name, company, business email, phone and the question itself. The paragraphs below are about the last field: what to put in it so the first reply is the answer rather than a request for details.

## Installation problems

Include:

- the app and its version (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote),
- how you installed it — which store listing, or whether an administrator deployed it,
- what happens instead of the assistant panel opening,
- whether other people in the same tenant see the same thing.

Common first checks are on [Install and first run](./getting-started.md).

## Subscription and billing

Include your plan name, how many users are on it, and whether you are asking about prompts, tokens or seats — these are priced separately. The current table is on [Plans and pricing](./pricing.md).

## Security review or procurement

Say which entity is contracting and which regulation your review is against, then ask for exactly what you need: the data processing agreement, the processing region for your tenant, the sub-processor list, or written confirmation of deletion practice. See [Compliance and certification](./security/compliance.md).

## Deleting data or an account

Data and accounts can be deleted at any time. Name the account and the records you mean, and say whether you need written confirmation for an audit file. Background: [What is stored and what is not](./security/data-handling.md).

# R2 Copilot for Word

In Word, R2 Copilot works beside the document: it drafts sections from an instruction, rewrites what you have, proofreads, offers alternative or expanded versions, and translates — without the document contents becoming training material.

**Get it:** Microsoft marketplace listing `WA200006000`, published by Stealth Mail. The same listing also covers PowerPoint and OneNote. Rated 4.8 out of 5 across 23 ratings. [Install steps](../getting-started.md).

## What it does

- **First drafts.** Turn an outline or a brief into a starting version you edit rather than a blank page.
- **Rewrites.** Ask for shorter, plainer, more formal, or aimed at a different reader.
- **Proofreading.** Corrections, plus alternative and expanded versions of a passage.
- **Translation.** Convert the text into the target language as you work.

## Who this matters for

The people who most need an assistant in Word are often the ones who cannot paste into a public chatbot: pleadings and judicial records, patient information, unpublished research, screenplays, financial reports. That is the case the product is built around — see [Use cases by role](../use-cases.md).

## Limits worth knowing

- Prompts and tokens come out of the shared monthly pool of your plan. [Plans and pricing](../pricing.md).
- Closing the panel ends the session and discards its content; put what you want to keep into the document first.

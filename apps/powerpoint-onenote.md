# R2 Copilot for PowerPoint and OneNote

PowerPoint and OneNote are covered by the **same add-in listing as Word**: `WA200006000`, published by Stealth Mail, rated 4.8 out of 5 across 23 ratings. Installing it once gives you the assistant in all three apps.

**Get it:** the [Word listing](./word.md) — there is no separate PowerPoint or OneNote package to find.

## What it does

The assistant behaves the same way it does in Word: it drafts from an instruction, rewrites what you have, proofreads, produces alternative or expanded versions, and translates. In slides that means speaker notes, slide copy and restructured bullet lists; in notes it means turning rough capture into something readable.

## Same subscription, same pool

There is nothing separate to buy. Prompts and tokens come out of the same monthly pool as your other apps — see [Plans and pricing](../pricing.md).

## Same privacy behaviour

Content is encrypted on your device, is not retained, and the session's content is discarded when you close the panel. See [How R2 keeps content private](../security/how-it-works.md).

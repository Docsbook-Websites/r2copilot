# R2 Copilot for Excel

In Excel, R2 Copilot builds formulas, explains what is happening between columns and suggests what the data shows — over spreadsheets you would not paste into a public chatbot.

**Get it:** Microsoft marketplace listing `WA200006007`, published by Stealth Mail. Rated 4.7 out of 5 across 21 ratings. [Install steps](../getting-started.md).

## What it does

- **Build formulas** from a description of the result you want.
- **Discover trends** and analyse dependencies between data sets.
- **Get recommendations** on what the numbers suggest, in natural language.
- **Explain someone else's sheet** — what a formula does and which cells it depends on.

## Why it matters that the sheet stays private

A spreadsheet is usually the most sensitive file in the room: customer lists, salaries, pricing models, forecasts. The privacy question is not theoretical, and it is the reason this add-in exists rather than a generic chatbot with a copy-paste box. See [How R2 keeps content private](../security/how-it-works.md).

## Limits worth knowing

- Prompts and tokens come out of your plan's shared monthly pool; long sheets consume more tokens than short questions. [Plans and pricing](../pricing.md).
- Closing the panel ends the session and discards its content.

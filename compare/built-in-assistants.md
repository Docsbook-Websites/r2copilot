# Choosing between R2 Copilot and a built-in assistant

If your office suite already ships an assistant, the honest question is not "which is better" but "which answers my organisation's questions". This page is the checklist to put to both vendors, with R2 Copilot's answers filled in so you can compare like for like.

## The checklist

| Question to ask | R2 Copilot's answer |
|---|---|
| Is my content retained after the session? | No. Content is discarded when the add-in closes and no chat history is stored |
| Is my content used to train or improve a model? | No |
| Is content encrypted before it leaves my device? | Yes, on the device |
| How is it protected in transit? | The patented SDNP protocol: packets split, mixed, reordered and repeatedly encrypted across independent nodes, plus Last Mile Protection |
| Where does the service run? | Microsoft Azure, built to the Azure Well-Architected Framework |
| Who is the publisher of record? | Stealth Mail, the company behind R2 Copilot |
| What certification is held? | Microsoft Solutions Partner, Industry AI on Azure |
| Which apps are covered? | Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Chrome, Telegram and web chat |
| How is it priced? | Per subscription with a prompt and token allowance, from $29 to $2,559 per month |
| Can we delete our data? | Yes, data and accounts can be deleted at any time |

## Where each option is usually the stronger fit

**A built-in assistant** tends to win when the value comes from reaching across the whole tenant — mail, files, calendar and chat at once — and when your legal review is already satisfied by the suite vendor's existing agreements.

**R2 Copilot** tends to win when the blocking question is the content itself: work you are contractually or legally unable to expose, teams that have been told not to paste into an assistant at all, and reviews where "not retained" is worth more than breadth of integration.

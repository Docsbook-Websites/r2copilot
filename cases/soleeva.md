# Soleeva Energy: faster campaigns, strategy stays inside

Soleeva Energy, a California solar technology company building anti-soiling, self-cooling panels, needed AI support for campaign work without putting internal strategy through external systems. In clean tech, the differentiators being discussed are the product.

> "R2 Copilot for Marketing transformed our marketing workflow. We no longer worry about exposing sensitive ideas to external platforms. It's like having a super-smart, NDA-signed strategist on our team."
>
> — Ahmad Qazi, CEO, Soleeva Energy Inc

## The problem

Brainstorming was fragmented and bottlenecked by manual drafting and revision. The available AI tools all expose data to external systems, which the team considered unacceptable while leading on clean-tech innovation.

## What changed

Campaign frameworks and creative variations now take minutes rather than sessions. The gain the team highlights is not only speed but alignment: marketing and engineering both contribute, and the assistant synthesises those inputs into coherent messaging.

The tools they rely on most are campaign ideation and message refinement — testing multiple copy versions and formats at once, with the assistant adapting to their brand voice. Discussions of product differentiators such as self-cooling panels stay in the session and go no further. See [What is stored and what is not](../security/data-handling.md).

## Results

- Campaign development time cut by **60%**.
- Four times the volume of creative concepts explored monthly.
- No premature exposure of ideas: every concept stays internal.

## About the company

Soleeva Energy is a California-based solar technology company, delivering anti-soiling, self-cooling solar panels.
